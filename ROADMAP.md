# Roadmap

## Product Direction

The app should become an effortless productivity companion, not a feature-heavy dashboard. The workday interface is the Dashboard HUD: current wRVUs, exams, pace, last sync, and review status. The full app is secondary and should answer questions, resolve exceptions, and configure automation.

Top-level navigation should follow workflows:

- Home: HUD, daily pace, timeline, goals, and current review state.
- Capture: PowerScribe clipboard/window capture and exception review.
- Analytics: study mix, accuracy, historical trends, and deeper explanations.
- History: completed logs and audit trail.
- Library: CPT/manual lookup tools.
- Settings: profiles, locations, automation, preferences, and data management.

## Near-Term

- Make the HUD the primary workday surface.
- Consolidate Daily Pace, Timeline, and Goals into Home.
- Consolidate Study Mix and Accuracy into Analytics.
- Move Automation, Profiles, Locations, and data configuration under Settings.
- Improve quiet PowerScribe clipboard/window capture with brief sync toasts.
- Persist active review sessions and exception queues reliably.
- Keep import review focused on low-confidence rows, duplicates, and unresolved matches.
- Search-first CPT Explorer polish.
